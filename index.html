Tricky Situations Cards

Cut-out cards for practising safe and helpful choices. A child may pass or choose another card.

SITUATION 1 OF 12

"Someone changes the rules because they are losing."

Think of Three — Use These 5 Questions With Any Card:
  1. What might each person be thinking or needing?
  2. What are three possible responses?
  3. What might happen after each response?
  4. Which response seems safest and most helpful?
  5. Who could help?
MENTOR BOUNDARY: Keep situations fictional. These cards help children practise judgement. Do not use them to investigate an actual incident. If a child shares a current safety concern, listen calmly and follow safeguarding procedures.

Mentor Quick Guide & Safeguarding

Kids Mentor Activity Toolkit (Ages 7–11) • REF-001-RA

Safeguarding: If a Child Tells You Something Concerning
  • Stay calm and listen. Take the child seriously.
  • Do not promise to keep it secret.
  • Do not investigate or ask leading questions.
  • Use the child’s own words when recording necessary information.
  • Follow current program, partner-school and Baptist Care SA procedures promptly.
A Useful Response Script: “Thank you for telling me. You have done the right thing. I may need to tell someone whose job is to help keep children safe.”
The Activity Is Not The Goal

Activities create a comfortable space for relationship, conversation and encouragement. The mentor is not acting as a teacher, therapist, counsellor or investigator.

School-neutral and faith-neutral child-facing approach.

What Helps in Every Session
  • Offer two or three activity choices.
  • Follow the child’s interests and pace.
  • Join in rather than interviewing.
  • Allow the child to pass on any question.
  • Notice effort, kindness, courage & persistence.
